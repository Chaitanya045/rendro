### Findings requiring follow-up

1. **Low — asymmetric narrow-screen drawer margin.** At 320px, the expanded dock measured left=16px, width=290px, right=306px: **16px left / 14px right**, rather than the documented 16px on both sides. `src/commentor/commentor.ts` `sizeContent()` calculates content width as viewport minus both margins; `repositionExpandedDock()` subsequently adds the dock's two 1px borders. Recommendation: include the outer border widths in the available-width calculation and add a real-browser narrow-viewport geometry regression. This did not cause viewport overflow in the tested dimensions.

This is an observed responsiveness issue, **not proof of its cause**; T3's concurrent snapshot failures may affect View Transition capture. Recommendation: reproduce with working capture, measure transition creation/update/ready/finished separately, and evaluate a safe immediate-theme fallback if capture stalls. Do not mark animation smoothness or no-flicker as passed.
